# Local character coverage review

Reviewed 2026-09-27. Dataset: `reviewed-2026-09-27.1`.

The registry contains 15 distinct characters: the original five plus ten
additions. Each has one role. These selections cover different stat needs;
they are not a popularity ranking or a claim of current-roster completeness.
Ownership does not filter matches, and eligible off-pieces remain candidates.
Manual profiles remain available.

## Source-use gate

This work is for the user's local, personal use only. It does not authorize
publication, redistribution, or a packaged derivative database.

The ten additions use individually checked KQM quick-guide sections. Each
guide carries a notice asking reference users to link the original. The
checked guides and [KQM site](https://keqingmains.com/) did not identify a
prohibition on this bounded personal-use factual summary. They also did not
establish a general redistribution license. The decision here permits only
short original records with links, not copied guide prose, images, tables,
spreadsheets, or bulk collection. No scraper, guide archive, runtime network
fetch, or external dataset was added. Attribution is not a license.

[Prydwen's terms](https://www.prydwen.gg/terms), marked March 2026 and checked
2026-09-27, permit personal non-commercial use and prohibit bulk extraction.
The existing small Furina record stays within this local review scope; no
concrete reuse issue required replacing its source. Its gameplay evidence
remains the original 2026-09-26 review, not a new gameplay review.

Disposition: all ten requested additions passed this limited local source-use

## Added role records

The following are original summaries of the linked sections read for this
review. All roles accept the fixed HP flower and ATK plume. Main choices are
independent eligibility checks, not complete loadouts. Crit means both Crit
Rate and Crit DMG. Core stats use the existing binary maximum-roll screen;
secondary stats have zero numerical weight.

### HP damage and ATK Burst roles

- **Neuvillette, on-field HP Charged Attacks.** HP% sands; Hydro or HP%
  goblet; Crit or HP% circlet. Core: HP% and Crit. Secondary: ER and flat HP.
  Marechaussee is preferred; Heart of Depth is a fallback. Droplets, HP-change
  stacks, Crit overcap, weapon buffs and HP-main combinations need build
  context. Triple HP% is not recommended by independent matching.
  [KQM: On-Field DPS and Artifacts](https://keqingmains.com/q/neuvillette-quickguide/),
  Luna IV; set calculations 5.8.
- **Yelan, off-field HP Burst damage.** HP% or ER sands; Hydro or HP%
  goblet; Crit or HP% circlet. Core: HP% and Crit; ER is secondary. Emblem is
  preferred; Nymph's Dream is a low-ER fallback. HP goblet and HP circlet are
  alternatives, not a combined recommendation. Normal Attack triggers and
  energy supply remain unknown.
  [KQM: Off-Field DPS, Artifacts and weapons](https://keqingmains.com/q/yelan-quickguide/),
  5.4.
- **Xingqiu, off-field ATK Burst damage.** ATK% or ER sands; Hydro goblet;
  Crit circlet. Core: ATK% and Crit; ER is secondary. Emblem is preferred;
  Noblesse is a conditional team-buff alternative. Normal Attack triggers,
  constellation and weapon procs affect uptime. No reaction ownership is
  assumed.
  [KQM: Off-Field DPS, Artifacts and weapons](https://keqingmains.com/q/xingqiu-quickguide/),
  5.7.

### Dendro reaction roles

  [KQM: Off-Field, Artifacts and A1/A4](https://keqingmains.com/q/nahida-quickguide/),

  [KQM: Artifacts, Quicken and mirrors](https://keqingmains.com/q/alhaitham-quickguide/),

  alternative. Deepwood is a conditional option without another holder.

### EM and healing support

- **Kazuha, EM Swirl support.** EM or ER sands; EM goblet and circlet. EM
  alone is core. ER and conditional Favonius Crit Rate are secondary.
  Viridescent requires on-field Swirl of the relevant element. Character
  level, aura, weapon and energy needs remain unknown. No Thundering Fury or
  carry variant is included.
  [KQM: Builds and support playstyle](https://keqingmains.com/q/kazuha-quickguide/),
  4.6.
- **Sucrose, EM reaction support.** EM sands, goblet and circlet; only EM
  is core. Optional-Burst ER and Favonius Crit Rate are secondary. Viridescent
  is preferred; Deepwood is conditional Dendro-team support. Favonius does
  not make a Crit circlet a preferred support main. Lunar-Charged Crit effects
  are outside this role.
  [KQM: Artifacts and EM support](https://keqingmains.com/q/sucrose-quickguide/),
  Luna VI.
- **Bennett, healing / ATK support.** ER or HP% sands; HP% goblet; Healing
  Bonus or HP% circlet. HP% screens healing only. ER, flat HP and conditional
  Favonius Crit Rate are secondary. Noblesse is preferred for ATK beneficiaries;
  Scroll is a reaction-dependent alternative. Artifact ATK never improves his
  base-ATK Burst buff.
  [KQM: Healing Support, sets and weapons](https://keqingmains.com/q/bennett-quickguide/),
  5.7.

Favonius utility descriptions are conditional application of weapon behavior,
not calculated proc odds. Binary weights and the core/secondary boundary are
this app's screening choices, not guide damage coefficients or endorsements.
For example, leaving Alhaitham's ATK% secondary does not mean it has no damage
value. ER needs always require a full build and rotation; no role scores ER
caps. Conditions remain `unknown`.

Excluded variants also include Neuvillette reaction-specific builds,
Yelan/Xingqiu on-field or Bloom, Fischl physical or non-Aggravate, Nahida
on-field/Nilou Bloom, Alhaitham non-Spread, Raiden Overloaded, and Bennett
damage/reaction-trigger builds. Existing pilot exclusions remain. Mixed
two-piece contexts, four-star Instructor pieces, unreviewed equipment and
all other characters are outside this pass. An unlisted set remains eligible
as an off-piece, without claiming its set bonus.

## Engine contract and limits

`CharacterRoles.reviewed` contains the original `pilot` plus the additions,
sorted by stable `characterID`. `pilot` still contains exactly its original
five records. `CharacterAssessment.evaluate` uses `reviewed` and returns all
eligible distinct characters in identity order. It does not truncate or rank
different-role raw scores. The evaluate signature and `CharacterMatch` result
shape are unchanged; UI shortlist selection belongs to the caller.

This work changes no calibration. New cells have no reviewed tiers and remain
ungraded until a separate calibration review. A missing tier is not D. HP%
healing and EM-only screens do not imply measured healing, team buff strength,
damage or build readiness.

When a main excludes the profile's only core substat, `potential` is nil with
an explicit reason. This covers EM-main Kazuha/Raiden/Sucrose pieces and HP%-main
Bennett pieces, as well as the existing Kuki/Kokomi cases. Saved copies cannot
produce guaranteed scalar rank ties for those pieces. A flower with no EM can
still have a valid zero EM score because EM is legal on that slot.

Ordinary roll math, missing-fourth-stat errors and unsupported Crafted/Dust
rules are unchanged. +20 yields actual values; unfinished pieces retain their
fixed-role P50, P90 and ceiling. Comparisons still use saved +20 pieces with
the same slot and exact main, selected set scope and linked-ID exclusion.
Counts, scope and partial-inventory coverage must remain visible in the UI.
This work adds no upgrade/discard action and reads no private inventory.
